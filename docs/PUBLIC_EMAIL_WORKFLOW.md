# Public email research

Create a role, enter the prospect's name and an official professional profile,
then choose Website search. Under Additional public sources, nominate up to
four public faculty, leadership, professional directory or publication pages.
Finding and selecting these pages remains a recruiter research step; the finder
does not discover every institution's other websites automatically.

All selected pages are validated before starting. Seeds run before suggested
links, sharing one five-page, 45-second budget. Each origin establishes its own
robots policy. Redirects remain within that seed's permitted canonical origin;
queries, credentials, LinkedIn, private network addresses and inaccessible
robots policies cannot widen the search. Excluded input sources are refused;
excluded linked or redirected content is blocked before its request.

Read the Name appears near this address section first. It reports a textual
hint from the normalized preceding passage or the address's own visible email
link, not ownership, identity or present reachability. Other published addresses
remain in collapsed source history. Historical searches without these hints
are labeled unassessed; saved review states and evidence are retained.

Every observation retains its URL, retrieval time, quote kind, normalized
context and SHA256 of the decoded page text. Website page receipts also retain
byte count and blocked/error/limit outcomes. A supported publication meta date
is recorded separately from retrieval time; publication correspondence is not
proof of a current recruiting inbox. Hashes make the retained observation
inspectable; they do not mean the complete original page is archived here.

Keeping a person is separate from confirming an email. All new addresses remain
pending with unknown deliverability. Pipeline export includes research source
URLs even while email review is pending. Email columns include only explicitly
approved, non-excluded observations. A later source exclusion blocks the linked
prospect's contact exposure, role actions and exports, including older saved
records; exclusions are sticky if the upstream feed later omits them. A failed
configured exclusion feed blocks actions until restored.

The local development pilot used five fixed physicians selected by specialty
and directory order for a publicly posted internal-medicine role. Primary-only
searches produced no prospect-specific address evidence. Adding manually
researched public sources produced useful named address observations for two
of five prospects. One source timed out, one publisher could not establish
robots rules, and one prospect had no email in checked sources. None was
replaced. Original negative runs were retained; no real-person association was
approved and no outreach was sent. This demonstrates a bounded workflow, not
held-out recall, current deliverability, candidate qualification, competitor
superiority or recruiting outcomes.

The platform uses no guessed address patterns, mailbox probing, paid enrichment
or external sending. Public-source availability and the need for manual source
nomination remain practical limits.
